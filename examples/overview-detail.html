Overview and detail

Drag the viewport to pan or drag either edge to zoom. Every control also works with a keyboard, and pinning an event brings it into view.

Full range

Pointer, pen, touch, and keyboard

Visible detail range

Viewport: Left/Right pans; Home/End moves to the bounds. Handles: Left/Right resizes; Home/End moves that edge. Hold Shift for one-minute steps.

Events in range

Hover or focus to preview; click, Enter, or Space to pin.
